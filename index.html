The shape of the trip

Not a map — just the loop, so you can see it in one look.

Days 1–8 · heading down

Inland through the lakes and passes to Queenstown, then west into Fiordland. Short hops, one or two nights each.

Days 9–13 · coming back

Back through Wanaka, over Haast Pass to the glaciers, up the coast, then over Arthur's Pass into Christchurch.

The one repeat

Te Anau back to Wanaka retraces the middle. Unavoidable — it's the only road north.

Three things to know

So nothing catches you out.

Haast Pass can close

It's the only road from Wanaka to the coast. Check the NZTA app the night before the 26th. If it's shut, the fallback is a second Wanaka night and Arthur's Pass straight to Christchurch.

September is shoulder season

Snow still on the passes, some walks closed, shorter hours everywhere. Upside: no crowds and no queues at Milford.

Don't book a scenic flight everywhere

Tekapo, Wanaka and Franz Josef all offer one, and all three is about $1,500 each. Franz Josef is the one worth it.
